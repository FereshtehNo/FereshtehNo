## About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Bioinformatics at the University of Tehran, working at the intersection of computational biology, structural bioinformatics, machine learning, and deep learning.

My research focuses on computational approaches for understanding, designing, and engineering proteins, with an emphasis on structure–function relationships, molecular modeling, and AI-driven protein science.

My current research broadly explores **de novo protein design and protein engineering of PETase-related enzymes using deep generative models**, with applications in sustainable biotechnology and plastic degradation.

I also develop reproducible computational workflows and bioinformatics pipelines using Python, Linux, and Google Colab.
